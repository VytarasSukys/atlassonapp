Optional Apple Watch companion

Leave the phone. Keep the walk.

The Watch app is a companion, never a requirement. Atlasson works on iPhone by itself, while Apple Watch can record an explicitly started walk and deliver it later.

Watch field protocol

Record first. Sync when the devices meet again.

Completed Watch walks remain local until the iPhone has fully committed the import. Temporary disconnection, a locked phone or being out of range does not make the walk disposable.

01. RecordStart a walk from the Watch when you want to leave the iPhone behind.
02. HoldThe completed walk stays on Watch until the phone acknowledges a committed import.
03. ContinueReconnect later and the walk joins the same local exploration history.
Optional by design.No Watch paired? No companion installed? Nothing on iPhone is blocked or degraded.

Local-first by design

Your atlas should belong to you.

Atlasson has no account system, social layer or developer-operated cloud sync. Walk history, exploration progress and Journey data are kept locally on your devices. Online map resources are fetched when the map is used.
