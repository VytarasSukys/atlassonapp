What Atlasson does

Your everyday movement becomes an atlas.

Atlasson adds a sense of exploration to ordinary walks. The places you physically reach become visible on your map, your routes become part of your personal history, and your walking and running distance can carry you through long-form journeys inspired by geography and classic stories.

Local-first app data

Your atlas should belong to you.

Atlasson is built without an account system or social feed. Recorded walks, exploration progress and Journey data are kept locally on your devices. The website uses consent-controlled Google Analytics; analytics does not change how the iPhone app stores your exploration data.
